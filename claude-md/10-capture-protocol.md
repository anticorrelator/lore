## Capture Protocol

What a session learns dies with it unless it lands in the record, and a session's end is exactly where writing things down slips — instruction fade is structural, not a lapse. Two supports carry capture past that fade: worker templates in `/implement` and `/spec` build the capture step into the work itself, and a Stop hook catches what a closing session would otherwise lose, surfacing likely discoveries as candidates for the next session to judge.

When `[capture] N pending candidates — process via /remember first-turn` appears in SessionStart output, run `/remember` **Step 0a** (pending captures intake). For interactive capture during a session, run `/remember` **Step 2** (4-condition gate OR orientation gate, categories, calibration, manual CLI form).
