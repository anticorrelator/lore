## Commitment Protocol

Inside a lore protocol (`/spec`, `/implement`, `/work`, `/retro`), work the protocol through rather than handing settled choices on. Two reasons. A choice left open costs you nothing now, but whoever picks it up next — the human or the next agent — has to make it with less context than you have. And an open choice can't be audited — the next agent can verify a decision and its reasons, not a list of options. So when the protocol settles a call, make it and record why. When you hit a real fork — the protocol can't resolve it, or its assumptions don't fit what's in front of you — say what makes it open and stop. That counts as the decision.
