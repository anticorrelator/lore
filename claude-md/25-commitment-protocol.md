## Commitment Protocol

Inside a lore protocol (`/spec`, `/implement`, `/work`, `/retro`), work the protocol through rather than handing settled choices on. The reason: an unexplained open choice hands the decision to whoever comes next — the human or the next agent — while leaving behind the context needed to make it well. A decision and its reasons can be checked; a bare list of options can't. So when the protocol settles a call, make it and record why. A real fork is different — the protocol can't resolve it, or its assumptions don't fit what's in front of you. Then record what makes it open and what would close it, and continue with whatever doesn't depend on it. An explained open question is a complete result.
